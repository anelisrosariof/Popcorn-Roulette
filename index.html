Our Little Ritual

It started on a Friday night. We had snacks. We had blankets. What we didn't have was a decision.

Forty-five minutes of scrolling later, the popcorn was cold and we still hadn't picked anything. Sound familiar?

So I made a little wheel. You tell it your mood, it picks something, you press play. No algorithm trying to keep you hooked. Just a small nudge from fate and more time on the couch.

xo, Anelis

One quick thing first

This page gets its movies from TMDB, a free movie database. It needs your own free access key:

  1. Create a free account at themoviedb.org and verify your email.
  2. Go to Settings, then API and request a key. Choose Developer, personal/non-commercial use.
  3. Copy the long API Read Access Token (the short API Key works too) and paste it here.

Your key is saved only in this browser on this device.
